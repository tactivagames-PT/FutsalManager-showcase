# Futsal Manager

> A futsal management game built from scratch, with **fictional** clubs and players and a 2D match engine.
> 🇵🇹 [Versão portuguesa](README.md)

![Live 2D match](docs/media/aovivo.png)

This repository is a **showcase only**. The game's source code is not here and the game is **not open
source** (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)). It exists to present the project and look for sponsors.

## What makes it different
- **Real futsal**: five-a-side lineups, rotations, flying goalkeeper (5v4), accumulated fouls, 10 m free kicks.
- **Portuguese leagues** as the starting point (fictional 1st and 2nd divisions), plus cups and an international competition.
- **2D engine**: matches play out on a top-down pitch and you can give orders while they run.

## Features
- **Live 2D match** with pause, 1×/2×/4× speed, in-game orders, replays, half-time and a final report with shot, heat and pass maps.
- **Tactics**: positional roles, individual and team instructions, set pieces.
- **Match preparation**: scout report on the opponent and man-marking.
- **Staff and training**: weekly plan, individual training, progress reports.
- **Advanced transfers**: clauses, loans, agents, shortlist and scouting missions.
- **Dressing room**: personalities, leaders, cliques and complaints.
- **Competitions**: Super Cup, League Cup, fictional international tournament, national teams and club ranking.
- **Board and finances**: sponsors, bank credit, financial fair play and club sale.
- **Multiple save slots**, light/dark theme and keyboard shortcuts.
